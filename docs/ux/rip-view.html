Rip's view

Rip's drawer during and after a run, at the real drawer size: half the canvas, run only, full width. Spec: docs/ux/rip-view.md.

  • One full-width composition, no side column. A band across the top holds the disc ring, the live readout and the read-speed trace. The track rows run below, at full width.
  • The disc: one arc per track, sized by length, filling as it's read.
  • The read speed: a scratch shows as a dip.
  • The rows: each track's proof: AccurateRip confidence, CRC, and the number of reads.
  • A track that needs you opens in its own row, with its two reads compared.
