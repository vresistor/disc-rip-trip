Cards and drawers, before and after

Revised after the owner's review. Spec: docs/ux/cards.md.

  • Version 7. Every card has the same five slots, each with one meaning: name, settings, outcome, one detail line, and one item strip. Every card is 288 × 192: a 3:2 card on a 16 px module, with 16 px margins and thirds on whole modules. Show grid, top right, draws the grid on every proposed card and the rhythm on the recipe.
  • The eye goes outline → outcome (20 px, the only large text, on the lower third line) → name → the rest.
  • Nothing short is cut: names, outcomes, settings values. Only long titles and deep paths shorten, with a tooltip.
  • Settings show as label and value (treatment A, compared below). They're not links; a click on the card opens its drawer.
  • The state is shown by the outline and the glyph.
  • The item strip is the same on every card, with one cell per item and one legend, and it's the only progress bar. Cover art also shows its cover.
  • Proposed drawers are shown at their default height: 2/5 of a 720 px window (268 px), with the canvas keeping 3/5. Today's are at half (330 px).
  • Each step shows only what came in on its wires, its own settings, and what it gave.

The CD recipe, end to end, mid-rip

Track 6 of 17 is being read. Both canvases are at 100 %; scroll sideways.

  • Today every tile is 152 × 112 px. Its status sits at the bottom, coloured by state.
  • Proposed, every tile is 288 × 192, in three bands:
    • the top third holds the name and settings;
    • the middle third holds the outcome, sitting on the lower third line;
    • the bottom third holds two detail lines and the picture on the bottom margin.
    Strips are a third of a card apart (64 px), and stacked branches a sixth (32 px).

One tile, every state: Rip

The slots never move. Before a run the card is its name and its settings, with one waiting cell. Once it runs, the outcome leads. The detail line says what it's on, and the strip shows which track is where: green verified, blue reading, orange to retry, red failed.

Every step, done

Every built-in step's tile after a disc. Each outcome is in its job's own unit, with a qualifier only where it changes what you'd think. Every card has the same five slots, and the strip uses one legend. A step that handles the disc as a whole shows a single cell.

Settings on the card: three ways

FLAC, MP3, Rip, Library and Transcode are each shown idle, changed by you, and locked while ripping. The settings slot doesn't move, so the card's grid is the same in all three.

A. Label and value (picked)

B. Quiet tags (two rows of tags fit the slot only at 14 px, so they're small and fiddly to click)

C. Edit mode only: not editing, then editing (as A, with the editing outline)

Picked: A. It reads as facts, not links, and so promises nothing it doesn't do. The label says what a bare 8 or slow means. The values sit on the first third line. C would hide configuration the design puts on the tile on purpose, and B brings back the chips you moved away from. A click on the card opens its drawer, where settings are edited.
