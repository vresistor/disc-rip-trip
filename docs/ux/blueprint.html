Blueprint cards

A step that hasn't run yet is drawn as a blueprint: a dashed border, a soft see-through ground, and only its name and settings. When it starts, it turns solid. The CD recipe in three moments, at 80 %.

1 · Ready, no disc

The whole recipe is a plan. Drive is the one card with something to say before a run.

2 · A disc is in

Drive has read the disc and turned solid; everything else is still the plan.

3 · Ripping

What has run is solid, with its results. What hasn't reached yet stays a blueprint, so you see how far through the plan it is.

  • Plan: dashed border, a soft see-through ground, idle ring, name and settings only. No empty strip, no cover placeholder.
  • Started: solid card on its ground; then the outline for working, needing you or failed, as now.
  • Wires into steps that haven't run are dashed and faint too.
  • Starting a new disc turns every card back into the plan.
